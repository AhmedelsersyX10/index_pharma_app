## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-trust, and reassuring digital environment for clinical healthcare, certified pharmacy services, and personal wellness logistics. The brand personality balances certified medical precision with hospitable warmth. Patients, caregivers, and health-conscious consumers must experience effortless cognitive clarity during high-stress interactions—such as refilling urgent prescriptions, reviewing dosage guidance, or ordering vital medical devices.

The design movement combines **Modern Clinical Minimalism** with soft **Tonal Layering**. Unlike harsh hospital software or generic e-commerce platforms, this aesthetic employs pristine luminous backgrounds, calming emerald and teal brand anchors, and micro-tinted mint container tiers. Structural borders remain whisper-quiet, prioritizing content scannability, legibility, and effortless touch navigation. The UI communicates unwavering efficacy, hygiene, and modern digital health standards.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around clinical integrity, instant legibility, and serene visual ergonomics.

- **Primary (`#00966B` / `#007A55`):** Deep emerald, signaling safety, clinical vitality, prescription reliability, and professional pharmacy heritage. Primary buttons, verified badges, active states, and clinical highlights draw directly from this core.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`):** Deep medical slate. Replaces stark pitch-black for all prominent typography, high-priority interactive buttons (e.g., "Add to Cart"), and solid contrast containers to maintain an authoritative, premium tone.
- **Tertiary (`#14B8A6`):** Vibrant teal for secondary calls-to-action, category accents, prescription refill progress bars, and informative indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Balanced cool slate gray for secondary captions, inactive icons, subtle dividers, and dosage helper metadata.
- **Mint Surfaces (`#F0FDF9` & `#CCFBF1`):** Soft antiseptic washes that isolate critical medical notices, authentic seal callouts, and badge backgrounds without overwhelming user focus.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers with **Geist** for functional content, clinical labels, and dosage descriptions.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Display):** Offers friendly, geometric curves balanced by architectural structure, giving the pharmacy an approachable yet unmistakably modern medical feel.
- **Geist (Body, Data, & Labels):** Provides neutral, uncompromising precision at small mobile sizes. Drug potencies (e.g., `500 mg`, `10 ml`), active ingredients, pricing, and serial numbers render with crisp optical fidelity.
- **Editorial Micro-copy:** Uppercase pill labels and category headers are styled with tighter tracking and medium/semibold weights to preserve clean scans across dense pharmaceutical listings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive mobile-first grid built on an strict 8pt rhythm, using 4pt increments (`space-2xs`, `space-xs`) for inline icon alignment, badges, and tag padding.

- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stack with dynamic 2-column product/category carousels. Canvas margin is pinned at `1rem` (16px) with an inner column gutter of `0.75rem` (12px) to maximize real estate for medical titles and dual-action buttons.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Fluid 8-column layout with `2rem` outer padding, comfortably displaying 3-to-4 product cards per row.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column bounded layout maxing out at `1280px`, anchored with `3rem` section margins.
- **Touch Targets:** Any interactive prescription action, incrementer, or navigation icon must respect a minimum touch target area of `44px × 44px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient clinical lighting** and **tonal containment** rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing spotless hygiene and medical transparency.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and slate tint `#F8FAFC`. Used for primary backgrounds and full-bleed reading surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Card & Tonal Surface):** Enclosed by a 1px border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` or mint boundary `#CCFBF1`, lifted with an ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Modules):** Applied to active product listings and category cards on tap or hover: `0px 10px 20px -4px rgba(0, 150, 107, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. A gentle emerald tint in the shadow suggests responsive physical feedback.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Modals):** Sticky prescription upload bars, mobile navigation, and dosage dialogs: `0px 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** geometry (0.5rem base radius). This produces an approachable touch feel without slipping into playful immaturity:

- **Cards & Medical Containers:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) ensures clean structural containment for medication images, pricing, and prescription warnings.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) creates clean, structured boundaries for drug search fields and quantity incrementers.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Chips:** Utilize full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for status indicators ("100% Genuine", "Free Delivery", "Prescription Required"), reinforcing the medicinal, capsule-inspired visual metaphor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Buy Now / Confirm Rx):** Background `#00966B`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape (`9999px`), bold label. Active states shift to `#007A55`.
- **Secondary Action (Add to Cart):** Deep slate `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape.
- **Prescription Upload Button:** Mint-tinted `#F0FDF9` surface with a 1.5px dashed or solid `#00966B` border, dark emerald typography, and prominent upload icon.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status & Trust Badges:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`. Light mint surface `#F0FDF9` with deep emerald text `#007A55` and a soft border `#CCFBF1`.
- **Offer / Discount Badges:** Bold amber `#F59E0B` or vibrant orange-red background with white bold typography for immediate urgency.

### Product & Medication Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white canvas, 1px subtle slate border (`#E2E8F0`), padding `12px` to `16px`.
- **Image Container:** Centered product photography set against a pure white or softly gray-vignetted background with an integrated favorite/wishlist button in the top corner.
- **Pricing & Actions:** Clear two-line hierarchy with prominent currency labels in bold slate, accompanied by stacked or side-by-side action buttons ("Add to Cart" and "Buy Now").

### Input Fields
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped or soft-rounded (`8px`), padded with leading search icon in primary emerald `#00966B`. Background `#F8FAFC`, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` with a 2px `#00966B` ring on focus.
- **Prescription & Dosage Selectors:** Segmented radios with clear checkmarks and tactile outline indicators.

### Trust & Review Modules
- Clean, vertical cards with pale mint quotation marks, verified purchaser indicators, and 5-star emerald or amber rating indicators.

## Flutter Implementation Architecture

All design tokens and component guidelines above are implemented in the Flutter codebase:

### 1. Token Files (`lib/core/theme/`)
- **Colors:** [`app_colors.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_colors.dart) — All brand emeralds, medical slates, mint washes, surfaces, and status colors.
- **Typography:** [`app_typography.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_typography.dart) — Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines/Display) & Geist (Body/Data/Labels).
- **Spacing:** [`app_spacing.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_spacing.dart) — 8pt spatial rhythm, 4pt increments, touch target rules (`44px`).
- **Shapes & Radii:** [`app_radii.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_radii.dart) — Rounded geometry (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`, and `9999px` capsule pill).
- **Shadows & Depth:** [`app_shadows.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_shadows.dart) — Level 0, Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 ambient clinical lighting.
- **Theme Extensions:** [`app_theme_extensions.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_theme_extensions.dart) — `ClinicalThemeExtension` for semantic clinical tokens accessible via `context.clinicalTheme`.
- **AppTheme:** [`app_theme.dart`](lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart) — Complete Material 3 `lightTheme` and `darkTheme`.
- **Barrel Export:** [`theme.dart`](lib/core/theme/theme.dart) — Single-import access to all tokens and themes.

### 2. Core Design System Widgets (`lib/core/widgets/`)
- **Buttons:** [`app_buttons.dart`](lib/core/widgets/app_buttons.dart) — `AppButton.primary`, `AppButton.secondary`, `AppButton.outlined`, and `AppButton.prescriptionUpload`.
- **Chips & Badges:** [`app_badges.dart`](lib/core/widgets/app_badges.dart) — `AppBadge.trust`, `AppBadge.offer`, `AppBadge.urgent`, and `AppBadge.neutral`.
- **Clinical Cards:** [`app_card.dart`](lib/core/widgets/app_card.dart) — `AppCard` and `AppCard.mint` with Level 1/2 ambient shadows.
- **Form Controls:** [`app_text_fields.dart`](lib/core/widgets/app_text_fields.dart) — `AppSearchBar` and `AppTextField`.
- **Barrel Export:** [`widgets.dart`](lib/core/widgets/widgets.dart).

### 3. Usage in App (`lib/main.dart`)
```dart
MaterialApp.router(
  theme: AppTheme.lightTheme,
  darkTheme: AppTheme.darkTheme,
  themeMode: ThemeMode.light,
  routerConfig: AppRouter.router,
);
```
